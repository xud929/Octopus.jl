H2 Deprecated spellings beyond the warning.
- `ThinStrongBeamSpec`: both public forms forward every keyword unchanged
  (206-208) to `_thin_strong_beam_spec`, whose body is the stage-1 typed
  constructor line for line (the diff renames the head only; 247-305);

  second (786-790); `offset_slices!` folds the waveform into the stored tuple

  with a nonzero stored tuple they differ by at most rounding, physics-level
  equal (the invariant asks for physics-level agreement). `mode = :replace`

  consistent with "resets the user's offsets").

  `offset_slices!(spec, (hoffset=..., ...); mode=:replace)` stores the same
  tuples plus the frozen `slice_center`/`slice_weight`, and the unnamed planes
