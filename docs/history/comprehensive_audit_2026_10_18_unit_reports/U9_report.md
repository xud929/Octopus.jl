### LEAD U9-2 [Minor, confidence high] src/tasks/strongstrong/gaussian_pic_cuda.jl:949-987
Claim: The two indexed gpic kick kernels are not grid-strided, yet they are launched through `_cuda_launch_capped!`, whose stated contract is "every kernel routed here is grid-strided, so this is a partition change, not a coverage requirement" (pic_cuda.jl:79-81); their plain-PIC twins are grid-strided, and the cap fires for the gpic longitudinal kernel at any `kick_threads` above 256.
Mechanism: `_cuda_gpic_kick_pair_indexed_longitudinal_kernel!` and `_cuda_gpic_kick_pair_indexed_kernel!` compute one `index` and run two `if index <= length(...)` bodies with no stride loop; `_cuda_pic_kick_pair_indexed_kernel!` (pic_cuda.jl:5032) and its longitudinal twin (5066) wrap the same bodies in `while index <= max(length(idx1), length(idx2)) ... index += stride`. Both gpic launches (816, 826) size `blocks = cld(max(length(idx1), length(idx2)), threads)` and hand the launch to `_cuda_launch_capped!`, which lowers `threads` to the kernel's launchable maximum and rescales `blocks = cld(blocks * launch.threads, threads)`. The rescale happens to preserve `blocks * threads >= n`, so no particle is dropped today (measured: with `kick_threads = 1024` the kernel is capped to 256 on all 18 launches of a two-turn task and the result matches the CPU at 1.3e-13 with 0 of 4000 particles off by more than 1e-9), but for these two kernels the rescale IS the coverage guarantee, which is exactly what the wrapper's comment says it is not. A later cap policy that bounds blocks (occupancy, a grid cap) or drops the rescale on the strength of that comment would silently skip 3/4 of the kicked particles on the hybrid only. Defect classes 4 (CPU/CUDA twin shape divergence, launch caps) and 14 (an invariant stated at the site that is false for two of its callers).
