### LEAD U41-1 [Moderate, confidence high] validation/twiss_xsuite_benchmark.jl:732
Claim: The gated C2 TOL-E "fitted order" on U2 and K2 is a pin on xtrack's finite-difference floor rather than a measurement of the Octopus integrator order, and it passes by 0.94% (U2) / 1.7% (K2) of its 0.3 band.

Defect classes 8 (a pin resting on rounding/floor) and 13 (an instrument that cannot see the quantity it gates). The suite's contract twin shows no ladder gate by grep (`ORDER_BAND`, `fitted_order`, `cap` absent from `src/contracts/twiss_external_reference.jl`), so the fragility is script-only, i.e. in the pre-push arm runs the README names.
