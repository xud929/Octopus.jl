### LEAD U16-2 [Moderate, confidence high] src/elements/strong_beam.jl:612-618
Claim: with `virtual_drift = :chromatic` the composed synchro-beam map `D^-1 B D` is not symplectic -- the residual max |J' S J - S| scales linearly with `kbb` (4.5e-9 at kbb = 1e-5, 4.5e-12 at 1e-8, 1.2e-12 at an EIC-like 2.6e-9, 1.4e-3 for a 5-slice beam at 1e-5) while `:hirata` and `:exact` sit at 1e-13 to 1e-16 in every branch; the docstring (147-148) and the ParamMeta promise all three are symplectic.
Mechanism: `_thin_strong_beam_track` hands `_cp_kick` the `S` the FORWARD drift computed from the IP coordinate, `S = (z_IP - zo)/2` (`_forward_virtual_drift(::ChromaticDrift)`, 719-728, returns it). The chromatic flow moves z (`z += 2 S phi`), so at the collision point `S(z*) = S (1 + phi)`, and the kick's potential `U(x*, y*; S)` with `S = S(z*)/(1 + phi(px, py, pz))` depends on the momenta: `Delta p = -grad U` is then not a canonical kick in CP coordinates, and the 1/2 in the longitudinal term (`pz += 0.5 * ...`) is not `dS/dz*`. The error is first order in `phi ~ (px^2 + py^2)/(4 (1 + pz)^2)` after the kick, hence proportional to `kbb` through the kicked momenta. Hirata leaves z alone (`S(z*) == S`), and the exact drift solves `z2 = (z + rr zo)/(1 + rr)` so that its `S` IS `(z* - zo)/2` (741-754) -- which is why both are symplectic and `:chromatic` alone is not. Recomputing `S = (z* - zo)/2` after the forward chromatic drift and handing THAT to `_cp_kick` restores the floor (2.1e-13 at kbb = 1e-5). The suite's "Virtual drifts: the named three are symplectic" uses central differences at step 3e-7 with tolerance 5e-7 and kbb = 1e-8 (residual there 4.5e-12), and the 2026-08-05 U7 check was FD-limited at ~1e-9, so neither could see it; the magnitude at realistic kbb is small but structural, and the public option's documented property does not hold.
