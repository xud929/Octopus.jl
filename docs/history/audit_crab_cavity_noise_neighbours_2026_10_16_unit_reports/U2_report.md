## 2 Leads

### LEAD U2-1 [low, confidence high] src/elements/crab_cavity.jl:58
Claim: The crab cavity's numeric type is decided by `frequency` alone (`T = typeof(float(frequency))` at construction, line 58, and again at compile, line 134), so a Float32 frequency silently narrows Float64 strengths and phases to Float32 and a non-Float64 strength is refused, while the RF twin promotes over every numeric parameter (`float(promote_type(...))`, rf_cavity.jl:303; `numeric_type(spec)`, rf_cavity.jl:357) and never narrows: twin drift in the Float32 promotion rules, pre-existing (not in the campaign diff) and argued nowhere I found (no ledger row names `float(frequency)`; the related owner item is the table-on-a-dual-cavity one).
Mechanism: `_harmonic_tuple(Val(N), values, T)` converts every entry with `T(values[i])`, so Float64 inputs become Float32 without a warning when `frequency` is Float32, and a complex-step or dual strength raises `InexactError` because `T` was fixed by the frequency (a complex frequency raises `MethodError`, so the crab is complex-steppable in no parameter). `ThinRFCavity(spec, method)` uses `numeric_type(spec)`, whose fold starts at `Float64` and widens over all numeric params, so Float32 inputs (friendly or raw spec) compile to a Float64 cavity and a complex strength to a `ComplexF64` cavity. The campaign's `_check_rule_numeric_type(s)` refusal inherits whichever `T` resulted: the same `TurnTable(Float64[...])` is accepted on a crab built from a Float64 frequency, refused ("a Float64 turn table on a Float32 cavity") on one built from a Float32 frequency, and accepted on every RF cavity. Measured: crab `ThinCrabCavitySpec{2}(1.0f8; strengthX=(1.0, 0.5))` stores `Tuple{Float32,Float32}` and compiles to `ThinCrabCavity{2,Symplectic6DMap,Float32}`; the raw RF spec with all-Float32 numbers has `numeric_type == Float64` and compiles to `ThinRFCavity{Symplectic6DMap,Float64}`; crab complex-step on `strengthX`: `InexactError` (friendly and raw); RF complex-step on `strength`: `ThinRFCavity{Symplectic6DMap,ComplexF64}`.
Repro: `julia --project=. -e 'using Octopus; s=ThinCrabCavitySpec{2}(1.0f8; strengthX=(1.0,0.5)); println(typeof(param(s,:strengthX)), " ", typeof(compile_runtime(s))); b=compile_runtime(ThinRFCavitySpec(400.8e6; voltage=12e6, e0=275e9, mc2=PMASS_EV)); r=ElementSpec{:thin_rf_cavity}(; frequency=400.8f6, strength=Float32(b.strength), beta0=Float32(b.beta0), gamma0=Float32(b.gamma0)); println(numeric_type(r), " ", typeof(compile_runtime(r)))'` prints `Tuple{Float32, Float32} ThinCrabCavity{2, Symplectic6DMap, Float32}` then `Float64 ThinRFCavity{Symplectic6DMap, Float64}`; the complex-step lines are in `probe_u2_attrib.jl`.

### LEAD U2-4 [info, confidence high] src/elements/lattice_magnets.jl:123  (SEAM, out of region: the shared drift the RF kernel calls for `L != 0`)
